Morning brief

This morning's global cues

Method. India-calendar panel: overnight markets enter through sessions that closed after India's previous close, and Asian markets through their opening gap. Ridge forecasts are refitted every 21 sessions on past data only. Hit rates are compared with majority and single-cue baselines, with Holm-Bonferroni-adjusted p-values across 16 tests. Spillovers use a VAR(1) on returns aligned to India's close and a generalised forecast-error variance decomposition (Diebold-Yilmaz 2012). Data: free daily index, futures and FX prices; unofficial and may contain errors. Not investment advice.
